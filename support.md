## Frequently asked

**How many people do I need to play?**
3 to 12. One person creates a room and shares the 6-character code (or a QR code, or a link) — everyone else joins with it.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Pick a codename and a color, and you're in. Nothing is tied to an email or phone number.

**Someone got disconnected mid-game — is their answer lost?**
No. If a player drops, their sealed answer stays in evidence and they can rejoin the same room for 30 seconds before their seat opens up.

**The host left — now what?**
The room offers the seat to the longest-serving remaining player automatically. No one needs to start over.

**Is there a way to report bad behavior in a room?**
Yes — any player can flag an exhibit during its reveal, and the host can remove a disruptive player from the room at any time.

**I want my data removed.**
BUSTED! doesn't use accounts, and nothing survives past your session on our server (rooms are deleted automatically after about an hour of inactivity). Anything saved on your own device — your codename, color, and settings — is removed the moment you delete the app. See our Privacy Policy (linked below) for the full picture.
